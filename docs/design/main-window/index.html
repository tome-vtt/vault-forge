    Obsidian setup

    What the converted notes need inside Obsidian. Install once per vault.

    • Admonitions Required

      Draws the call-out boxes for rules text, spells and traits. Without it they show as raw code.

      Open in Obsidian
    • Fantasy Statblocks Recommended

      Draws monsters as statblocks. Needed when "Statblocks for Fantasy Statblocks" is on.

      Open in Obsidian
    • Dice Roller Recommended

      Makes attack and damage rolls clickable. Needed when "Clickable dice" is on.

      Open in Obsidian
    • Initiative Tracker Recommended

      Runs encounters with the converted monsters.

      Open in Obsidian

    Import the call-out styles

    1. In Obsidian, open Settings, Community plugins, Admonition, then Options.
    2. Press Import and choose admonitions-5e.json.

    Point Fantasy Statblocks at the notes

    1. In Fantasy Statblocks' settings, turn on "Automatically Parse Frontmatter for Creatures".
    2. Set Bestiary Folder to compendium/bestiary.
    3. For Initiative Tracker, turn on "Sync Monsters from TTRPG Statblocks" under Plugin Integrations.

    Turn on the style snippets

    1. Copy the snippet files into your vault's .obsidian/snippets folder.
    2. In Obsidian, open Settings, then Appearance.
    3. Under CSS snippets, press the refresh button and turn each snippet on.
